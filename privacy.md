# Zexo — Privacy Policy

**Last updated: October 2026**

## The short version

Zexo collects **nothing**. Your data never leaves your device.

## What Zexo stores

All information you enter — expenses, budget settings, paycheck records,
goals, streaks — is stored **locally on your device only**, using the
operating system's standard app storage. There are no Zexo servers, no
accounts, and no sign-in.

## Paycheck text and PII

If you paste pay-stub text, it is scanned **on-device** to detect and redact
personal information (SSNs, account and routing numbers, emails, phone
numbers, dates of birth, addresses, names). Only the redacted copy is kept.
The original text is held in memory during the scan and discarded immediately.

## What Zexo does not do

- No analytics or tracking
- No advertising or ad identifiers
- No third-party data sharing (there is nothing to share)
- No sale of personal information

## Data deletion

Uninstalling the app permanently deletes all of its data from your device.
Because no data ever reaches a server, there is nothing else to delete.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page.
